================================================================================
WALKTHROUGH STEPS (to be filled as we go)
================================================================================
Step 1: Offset distribution (arrival spread) from historical entries
Step 2: Dwell survival S = (offset - b)
Step 3: Demand / arrivals curve (forecaster output)
Step 4: Baseline occupancy = arrivals (convolved with) S
Step 5: Near-term occupancy = still-present (scans) + max(forecast, reservations)
Step 6: pct + seat_probability + confidence
Step 7: Scoring API slot reduction
